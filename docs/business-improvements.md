### Cost approval

1. List production and contractor costs by deliverable.
2. Estimate staff hours including account management, QA, revisions, and reporting.
3. Use a loaded hourly cost, not just salary divided by working hours.
4. Allocate tools and confirm referral commission rules.
5. Estimate acquisition cost and paid retention; test shorter retention as a downside scenario.
6. Review contribution against general overhead, setup effort, and available capacity.

The workbench computes monthly contribution = service fee − contractor cost − staff hours × loaded rate − allocated tools − recurring commission − acquisition cost / retained paid months. It is not net accounting profit. Setup work, refunds, taxes, and general overhead require a separate review.

## Consultation and qualification

**Suggested calendar title:** Koloss Media Growth Consultation.

**Suggested description:** “A 30-minute conversation about your audience, offer, and marketing goals. We’ll review your current activity, identify the biggest obstacle, and discuss a useful starting point. Please bring your website or social profile and your main goal. Advertising spend is separate from service fees.”

**Suggested intake questions:**

1. Business owner, personal brand, or both?
2. Website / main social profile?
3. What do you sell, to whom, and at what typical price?
4. Which outcome would make the next 90 days successful?
5. Current monthly service and advertising budgets?
6. Who approves the engagement, and when can you start?

**Call structure:**

- Minutes 0–5: offer, audience, and current situation.
- Minutes 5–15: current marketing, conversion path, bottleneck, baseline, and capacity.
- Minutes 15–23: recommended scope, responsibilities, expected process, and price fit.
- Minutes 23–30: questions, decision maker, proposal requirements, next action, and owner.

Qualification means relevant need, fit with a deliverable we can perform, an attainable budget, access to a decision maker, and a realistic timeline. If these are unclear, record the gap and next discovery action instead of forcing a proposal.

The prepared consultation page creates a local brief and links to the live site's existing calendar at `https://cal.com/ivans-qbhhss/30min`. Changing the calendar title, availability, or intake settings requires access to that calendar and is not completed in this release.

## Weekly operating review

Hold one short review with Ivan, the sales owner, and delivery lead. Record:

- Acquisition: source, inquiries, qualified leads, calls booked and attended, proposals, paid clients, and acquisition spending.
- Delivery: work planned/completed, hours, revision count, late approvals, and unresolved issues.
- Economics: collected service revenue, direct costs, recurring referral costs, contribution, and capacity.
- Retention: active clients, renewals, cancellations, reasons, and account-specific next action.

Measure funnel rates by a consistent lead cohort. Define conversion events before comparing periods. The workbench hides rates when later stages exceed earlier ones and leaves missing or undefined rates blank.
