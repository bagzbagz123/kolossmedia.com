BEFORE YOUR CONSULTATION

A useful
starting point.

Can we start with footage we already have?

Bring a sample or public link to the consultation. We’ll discuss its suitability and define whether the scope uses existing material, new production, or both.

Is this a fixed monthly package?

Content sits within the Custom option. It can be a focused project or a tailored monthly plan. Formats, quantity, schedule, revisions, and fee are confirmed in writing before work begins.

What should I bring?

Your website or main social profile, a clear description of your offer, examples of existing content, and the main problem you want to solve. If you have a budget or deadline in mind, include it in the optional consultation brief.
