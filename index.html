Before we talk

A few good questions.

Do I need to know which package I want?

No. Bring your business goal and current challenges. We’ll use the consultation to identify a useful starting point.

Is advertising spend included?

No. Media spend is separate from the management fee. We’ll agree on a budget and responsibilities in the proposal.

Can you help with just content or a website?

Yes. The custom option lets us scope a specific project or content engagement without bundling services you don’t need.

What do you need from my team?

Your offer, brand materials, relevant account access, a point of contact, and timely approvals. Filming and production responsibilities depend on the agreed scope.

Do you guarantee sales or follower counts?

We agree on the work, measurement, and review process. Sales and audience growth depend on factors beyond any one campaign, so we don’t promise fixed counts.
