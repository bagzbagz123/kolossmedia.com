A few good
questions.

What happens in the consultation?

We’ll spend 30 minutes discussing your offer, audience, current marketing, and priorities. The aim is to identify a useful starting point and what a proposal should cover.

Can we start with one specific piece of work?

Yes. Bring the problem you want to solve, whether it involves content, a campaign, search visibility, or your website. We’ll discuss a focused scope or a more connected plan.

Do you work with personal brands?

Yes. We work with businesses and personal brands. The starting point is your offer, the people you want to reach, and the action you want them to take.

What do you need from my team?

A clear picture of your goals, relevant brand materials, and access to the accounts involved. We’ll agree on approvals and responsibilities before work begins.

What should I prepare for the call?

Your website or main social profile and the one thing you most want to improve are enough to start. Preparation is optional; you can add useful context directly in the calendar notes.
